## 1. As the lay reader

### The plain page

**Where I got lost.**
- "There is one reply inside it. And there is a single step of computation, the thing that picks the next word." I can follow this, but the difference between "one reply" and "a single step" is invisible to me until finding 5 ("Inside a single step"), and by then I have forgotten that a reply is many steps. - "seven rows, one per way of looking: measuring inside the model, watching what it does, asking what it says about itself, checking where those three disagree, two kinds of theory about what would make any of it matter, and what a person actually meets." That is seven things in one sentence; I counted twice. The "two kinds of theory" are never named, so when finding 1 says "The row about whether anything is felt" I do not know it is one of those two.
- Finding 2: "the measurement rises for the email's author; when the model writes its own reply, it tracks the reply." Rises "for" the author? I read it three times. I think it means "the needle moves while the model is reading the author's desperate words." Say that.
